What to expect

  • Your contact details and saved answers are filled in by rule on this computer. The LLM reads the form's questions as text (never screenshots) and sees placeholders for your details.
  • It stops and asks when it isn't sure, when a site wants you to sign in or create an account, for CAPTCHAs, and when the application moves to another website.
  • Diversity questions are answered with "prefer not to say" when offered, otherwise left to you. Consent boxes are yours to tick; job-alert boxes stay unticked.
  • It never submits by itself. Open the nerdbar (`) to watch every step.
