What helps a CV rank

  • One achievement per line, with a number where you have one (time saved, users, cost, size): ranking systems and recruiters favour measurable results.
  • Name skills the way job postings do (“Kubernetes”, “Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)”): the app matches other names too, but exact words rank best.
  • A start (and end) date on every role, and your education: applicant tracking systems look for both.
  • Only facts you'd stand behind in an interview: everything here is treated as true.
